You'll play a coordinator who receives six client estimates in a row and turns each one into a project. After each one you check that the app planned it sensibly. You can't break anything: it all runs in a practice copy of the app.

How every scenario page works
DoNumbered steps. Do them top to bottom. Purple words are buttons or tabs to click; grey boxes are text to type. CheckWhat you should see. Tick each box. Write downCopy one or two dates from the screen. OptionalAn extra test. Skip it if you're short on time.
Five words you'll see
  • Day 1: the first weekday on or after the day you're testing. If you test on a Tuesday, Day 1 is that Tuesday; Day 2 is Wednesday.
  • Best-fit: give each task to the most skilled person, even if they're busy.
  • Fastest: give each task to whoever can finish it soonest.
  • Work key: what a person is allowed to do, e.g. "IT Level 2". Level 2 is senior and can also take Level 1 work; Level 1 can't take Level 2.
  • "after 17h already booked": that person had 17 hours of earlier work, so this task waits behind it.
Setup · do this once

Get the practice app ready

Four short stages. When you finish D, everyone on the team is free and set up the same way the expected results assume.

A

Open the practice copy

Why not the real app? In the real app, starting a project creates a real deal in Zoho and a real purchase order in billing, and billing can't delete one. The practice copy (called Demo) plans exactly the same way but keeps everything in your browser.

  1. If the app is already running in a terminal, stop it (click the terminal, press Ctrl + C).
  2. Open Git Bash in the project folder (not PowerShell) and paste:
    NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL= NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY= npx next dev -p 3001
  3. When it says "Ready", open http://localhost:3001/dashboard in Chrome.
  4. Top left, next to "Timeline Estimator", the badge must say Demo. If it says Live, close the tab and ask for help.
B

Empty the sample data

  1. Bottom left, in your name card, change the role dropdown to Admin.
  2. Click Projects. Delete all four sample projects: open each one's ⋯ menu → Delete → confirm.
  3. Click Workload → Team. Everyone should show 0 h of open work.
  4. Click Settings → Time off → Team time off. Delete both sample entries (Martin's "Annual leave" and Arvin's "Morning only").

Read once before scenario 1

Three things you'll do in every scenario

1 · Create an estimate and add its lines
  1. Estimator → New estimate. Type the title and client from the scenario page; platform stays WordPress. Click Create and open.
  2. You land on Line items. There's one empty starter row: use it for the first line.
  3. For each line: type the title, open the row's details, set Role and Base hours. Leave Tier on Basic and Qty on 1.
  4. For the next line, click Add row and repeat.

A row without a title doesn't become a task, so don't leave the starter row empty.

2 · Look at the plan
  1. Click the Adjust tab. Find the card Who does the work.
  2. Pick Best-fit or Fastest, as the scenario says. The card shows the launch date.
  3. Click See who does what: every task with its person and dates.
3 · Turn it into a project
  1. Top right, change the status to Approved.
  2. Click Start project → Review schedule. It should match what you saw on the Adjust tab.
  3. Click Confirm & create. The project now exists, and its people are busy for the next scenario.
Glance at these every time

You don't need to write these down. Just stop and note it if one looks wrong.

  • No task starts or ends on a weekend or a holiday.
  • One person never has two tasks at the same time.
  • QA (testing) comes after the building work, not before.
  • IT Level 2 work only ever goes to Daniel or Patrick.

Something looks wrong? Write down the scenario number, the person, the task and the dates shown, and take a screenshot of See who does what. Then carry on with the next step.
