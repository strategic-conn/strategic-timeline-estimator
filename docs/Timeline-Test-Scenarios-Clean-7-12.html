Maple Dental: a holiday turned into a working day

You're checking that when an admin marks a holiday as a working day, plans use it, and go back when it's undone.

Do

  1. New estimate: title S7 Maple Dental, client Maple Dental.
  2. Scope & risk → Start guided scoping. Section 2: 6 pages, 2 templates, everything else No. Untick sections 4, 5, 6 and 8.
  3. Section 7: Project Start Date 2027-01-11, review turnaround 0.
  4. Generate line items → Adjust → Best-fit. Open the Timeline tab and write down the launch date (A).
  5. Settings → Workspace → Holidays: set Martin Luther King Jr. Day to Working. The page reloads.
  6. Go back to the estimate's Timeline tab. Write down the launch date (B).
  7. Back in Holidays, set MLK Day to Day off again. Check the estimate: write down the launch date (C).
  8. Approve → Start project → Review schedule → Confirm & create.

Check

  • At step 4 the chart mentions Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Mon Jan 18), and nothing is dated on it.
  • B is one working day earlier than A.
  • C is the same as A.
  • In Review schedule, no task is dated Jan 18.

Write down

A · MLK Day off
B · MLK Day worked
C · back to Day off
Result:PassFail

Willow School: left-out sections and extra safety time

You're checking that left-out sections disappear from the timeline, and that the risk buffer moves dates but never the price.

Do

  1. New estimate: title S10 Willow School, client Willow School.
  2. Scope & risk → Start guided scoping. Section 2: 8 pages, 3 templates, everything else No. Untick only sections 4 and 6.
  3. Section 7: Project Start Date 2027-02-01, review turnaround 2.
  4. Generate line items. Open the Timeline tab.
  5. Adjust → set Risk buffer to 0%. Write down the launch date and the price (top right).
  6. Set Risk buffer to 20%. Write down both again.
  7. Back in the questionnaire, tick section 4 on: a Content bar appears. Untick it again.
  8. Keep 20%, pick Best-fit. Approve → Start project → Review schedule → Confirm & create.

Check

  • The timeline has no Content and no SEO bars, and skips Presidents' Day (Mon Feb 15).
  • At 20% the launch is later, and the chart says "incl. 20% risk buffer".
  • The price is exactly the same at 0% and 20%.
  • The new project has no Content or SEO sprint, and its own risk buffer shows 0% (the 20% is already built into its dates).

Write down

Launch at 0%
Launch at 20%
Price at 0%
Price at 20%
Result:PassFail
